# ACP-Extension  
## ACP (Antichesspropaganda)

ACP (Antichesspropaganda) is a browser extension built strictly for analysis purposes on:

- https://www.chess.com
- https://lichess.org

This extension is designed to assist with post-game study, position review, and instructional exploration.

---

## ⚠️ Fair Use & Ethical Notice

ACP is for analysis and educational purposes only.

Do NOT use this extension:

- During live games
- To gain an unfair advantage
- To bypass fair play systems
- For cheating in any form

Using external assistance during active games violates the Terms of Service of both Chess.com and Lichess and may result in account suspension or permanent bans.

You are solely responsible for complying with platform rules.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

ACP aims to:

- Support deeper understanding of chess positions
- Encourage experimental analysis
- Assist in post-game review
- Promote strategic development

This project exists to improve players — not to undermine fair competition.
---

## 🛠 Installation

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open your browser's Extensions page.
3. Enable Developer Mode.
4. Click "Load unpacked".
5. Select the ACP project folder.
